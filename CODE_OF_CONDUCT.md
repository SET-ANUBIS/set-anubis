# Code of Conduct

SET-ANUBIS follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the scientific usefulness of the
  project;
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, sexualized language or
imagery, trolling, insulting comments, public or private harassment, and
publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported through
GitHub issues or directly to the maintainers. Maintainers are responsible for
clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this Code of
Conduct.
